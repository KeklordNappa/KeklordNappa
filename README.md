### Welcome!

My name is William. I'm an independent, full-stack video game and simulation developer and the founder of [Summoner's Guild Games](https://github.com/Summoners-Guild-Games/). I have experience in almost every development role on a game and simulation team, including programming, systems design, graphics design, UI/UX, narrative design, and even a smattering of music production. I have contributed to multiple small-scale games in the past, such as [_Cavitation_](https://globalgamejam.org/games/2025/cavitation-2)_,_ made for Global Game Jam 2025, where I acted as a programmer, game designer, and artist. I am also active in the video game modding scene, with a particular interest in projects that focus on modifying and uplifting older games by providing new and interesting ways to play, such as the [Archipelago](https://archipelago.gg/) project.

Graduate of the University of Tulsa, B.Sc. in Computer Simulations and Gaming. Minors in Computer Science and Art.

**What I'm working on now:** A special, super secret video game! It won't be a secret for long, though, so check back soon!
